Your devices, together

Send text, photos and files between your own devices on the same local network.

After installation, LocalClipboard opens a registration wizard. Choose Русский or English there, then create a personal profile or enter your connection code.

Each person needs their own profile, even on the same Wi-Fi.

Run this same installer again to reinstall or remove LocalClipboard. On the next page, select Remove LocalClipboard to uninstall instead of installing. Your profile and files are kept.
